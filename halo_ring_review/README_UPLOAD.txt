UPLOAD STRUCTURE
================

Upload the complete folder `halo_ring_review` to the repository root:

halo_ring_review/
├── index.html
└── assets/
    ├── frame/
    │   ├── halo_ring_back.webp
    │   └── halo_ring_front.webp
    └── animals/
        ├── panda.webp
        ├── wolf.webp
        ├── lion.webp
        ├── dragon.webp
        └── owl.webp

Then open:
https://dashboard-izin.pages.dev/halo_ring_review/

IMPORTANT
Do not upload only index.html.
Keep assets exactly inside halo_ring_review/assets/ because index.html uses
relative asset paths.
